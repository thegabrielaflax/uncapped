# What's new in Uncapped

## 0.4.0
- **A new look for the whole panel.** Calmer, clearer screens with one next move at the top of each, your numbers at a glance, and fewer things to open and close.
- **Home.** Once you've started, the panel opens on Home: your next move, how your outreach and posts stand, your offers and your path.
- **Discover.** Each answer you've given is a tile; open one to read it in full or change it in the chat.
- **Your offer.** Your Portfolio Garden in three views: your pick, all your ideas as cards you can filter by way of earning, and how the five ways to earn compare.
- **First 10 Customers.** Your list, each person's page with their message, and a toolkit with your call guide.
- **A new Substack strategy.** Before it's written, Claude asks a few questions in the chat about your stories and your views, and keeps what you say so you're never asked twice. Uncapped then researches your field, chooses ten posts with an editor's read, researches each post, and writes and checks a full card for every one: who it's for, what they're stuck on, where your offer comes in, the idea, how it unfolds, what research adds (with real links), what's from you, what you'll need to add, and how it ends, ready to copy. It takes about 12 minutes, and your whole strategy appears together when all ten cards are finished. If it stops, one button, Finish my strategy, carries on from where it was. "Plan this post" writes a line-by-line plan for when you sit down to write, "Change this idea" changes one post with you in the chat, and Strategy has a footer for every post. A strategy you built before keeps its own screens.
- **Your work is unchanged.** Everything you saved before opens exactly as it was.

## 0.3.3
- **Updates reach every copy.** The Update button now updates Uncapped everywhere Claude might use it, so your next new chat is always on the new version.

## 0.3.2
- **Updating in one press.** When a new version is ready, an "Update to" button appears above the message box. Press it, then start a new chat. The Updates button in the panel works too.

## 0.3.1
- **Opening Uncapped.** Sending "Open Uncapped" opens the panel and Claude replies with your next step, instead of a "blocked" note.

## 0.3.0 — first release
- **Discover.** A short interview in the chat about your work, what people ask you for and the week you have. Talk instead of typing if you like (Wispr Flow, Willow Voice or Claude's own dictation). The panel shows what's saved and how many questions are left.
- **Your Portfolio Garden.** Eight to twelve ideas you could get paid for, drawn from your own experience, shown as cards. Open any idea to see what you'd do, what the buyer gets, who'd buy it and when, a price and time to test, and how to test it first. Uncapped's pick shows where it would start.
- **Start with one offer.** Choose an idea and build its two plans from the top of your garden.
- **First 10 Customers.** Ten people or routes to them, your first move, and a first message for each, written with you in the chat in your own voice. Once two messages are saved, "See what to ask" gives you a guide for your calls. Each person has a record you can update as things move.
- **Your Substack strategy.** Who you're writing for, their week, series to come back to, and ten posts in order: an intro story first, then methodology posts and audience builders. Every post has a full brief: the idea, where to open, the story beat by beat, and where to land it. Notes are ready to copy.
- **After your first conversations,** tell the chat what happened. Uncapped saves it and helps you decide what to change.
- **More than one kind of buyer?** Offers for different buyers keep their own plans and Substack.
- **Your work stays yours.** Everything is saved in your Uncapped folder. Updates never change it.
- **Help.** Feedback or help in the panel prepares a note to Gabriela as a draft in your email app.
