---
name: open-pages
description: Open the person's Uncapped pages in their browser - everything they have built, as a small website on their own computer. Use when they say "open my pages", "open Uncapped", "show me my garden", "show me my offer", "where is my work", "can I see it all in one place", or come back after a break and want to look at what they have.
---

Call the **`open_pages` tool**. It grows the pages from their saved work and
opens them in their browser, and it returns what to tell them.

It is a tool, not a shell command. Never run `uncapped-pages` in a terminal:
that is a slash command for the person to type, and it does not exist in the
shell.

Then say, in one short line, that their pages are open in their browser. If the
tool says it could not open them, give them the file path it returns and tell
them to open that file in their browser.

Two things to know, so you never say something wrong:

- **The pages are files on their computer.** There is no website and no
  account. They work with no internet.
- **Nothing is pressed on the pages.** Each page shows the exact words to say,
  and the work happens here in the chat. When you finish something, the pages
  are rewritten straight away, so they only need to refresh their browser.

Never say where the pages are on their screen. Their browser may be beside
Claude or on another monitor. Say "in your browser", never "beside this chat".
